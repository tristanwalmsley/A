Our first course

The University of Besties™ was founded in 2026 with one programme, and we have given it our full attention.

BA (Hons) · Level 4 · Online

British Studies

Geography, history, institutions and everyday etiquette, then two deep dives into the Red Rose and White Rose counties. You will learn what the difference between Britain, the UK and England really is, how to make a proper brew, and why you should never say “barm” to the wrong person.

Taught in twelve short lectures, each ending with a check-your-understanding quiz.

Apply for this course

At a glance

  • 3 modules, 10 credits each
  • 12 lectures, 4 assessments
  • Classified in line with the UK degree system
  • School of Biscuits & Brewing

What you could become

A person who can explain a queue, a pasty and a proper “not bad” to anyone, anywhere.

Modules

Taken in order. Each module ends with an exam that must be passed before the next one opens.

BT1001 Introduction to Britain

10 credits · Exam 1 · 20%

The foundation for everything else: geography, history, institutions and everyday culture, including the often-misunderstood difference between Britain, the UK and England.

  1. L1 The Lay of the LandGeography, the four nations, counties and the North/South divide
  2. L2 A Very Quick HistoryRomans to the modern day
  3. L3 How Britain WorksMonarchy, Parliament, the NHS
  4. L4 Everyday BritainTea, queuing, pubs, sarcasm, and what “not bad” really means

By the end you can

  • Distinguish the four nations
  • Outline the key periods of British history
  • Interpret everyday etiquette and idiom

Assessed by

  • Make a Proper Brew (practical)
  • What Would a Brit Say? (exam)

BT1002 Lancashire

10 credits · Exam 2 · 20%

The Red Rose county: its borders and identity, its central role in the Industrial Revolution, its food, football and folk traditions, and its dialect.

  1. L1 The Red Rose CountyBorders, the Duchy and Lancashire Day on 27 November
  2. L2 Cotton, Mills & the NorthHow Lancashire powered the modern world
  3. L3 Food, Football and FolkHotpot, Lancashire cheese, Eccles cakes
  4. L4 Speaking LancashireDialect, and the correct use of “love”

By the end you can

  • Describe the historic county and its identity
  • Explain the role of cotton and the mills
  • Translate common Lancashire dialect

Assessed by

  • Spot the Lancastrian (practical)
  • Dialect Translation (exam)

BT1003 Yorkshire

10 credits · Exam 3 · 20%

The White Rose county: the largest historic county in England, its rivalry with Lancashire, its food and traditions, and its dialect, treated comparatively.

  1. L1 The White Rose CountyThe Ridings, the Dales, Yorkshire Day on 1 August
  2. L2 The Great RivalryFrom the Wars of the Roses to modern-day banter
  3. L3 Puddings, Tea and PrideYorkshire pudding, Yorkshire Tea, local pride
  4. L4 Speaking YorkshireDialect, and how it compares with Lancashire

By the end you can

  • Describe Yorkshire and its identity
  • Explain the Wars of the Roses and modern rivalry
  • Compare Lancashire and Yorkshire culture

Assessed by

  • Pick a Side (practical)
  • Yorkshire dialect (oral exam)

How you’re assessed

No essays. Three module exams worth 20% each, then a final exam worth 40%. Feedback after every assessment, and resits are supported with additional biscuits.
