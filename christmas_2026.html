UK vs NL Christmas

two countries, two ways to do the holidays

🇳🇱 Sinterklaas first

The big gift night is 5 December, with poems and a shoe by the fireplace. Christmas comes after as a cosy second round.

🇬🇧 Crackers & mince pies

Paper hats, terrible jokes, a roast dinner and a King's speech nobody quite watches.

🇳🇱 Two Christmas days

Eerste and Tweede Kerstdag - 25 and 26 December - are both public holidays, so there's no rushing it.

🇳🇱 Oliebollen

Deep-fried doughnut balls with icing sugar, eaten on Oud en Nieuw (New Year's Eve). Stroopwafels are available all winter.
